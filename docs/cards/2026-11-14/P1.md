# P1 · Plan-quality rubric from Sarah Chardonnens's framework (draft for the owner)

## Why

At the 29 October committee meeting Sarah Chardonnens (University of Fribourg) asked what learning theory Silicus's
lesson plans rest on. Honest answer: almost none; the plan prompt in `host/worker.mjs` contains no pedagogy. She then
shared her booklets. Before we change the prompt (card P2, later), we need a rubric to score plans before and after,
so "we added learning theory" is a measured claim, not a slogan.

## Read

- All five booklets in `/Users/bonorinoa/Hermes/Projects/silicus-private/sarah/` (`.docx`; your file reader extracts
  them). The Core Booklet first: the four SYNAPSE phases and the motivational dynamic, "who does the work of
  learning?", the three AI statuses (forbidden, permitted, required), the three-question delegation grid, the autonomy
  scale by age, "one piece of evidence per session", spaced review. Then the four Level Booklets for what changes by age.
- What a Silicus plan contains today: the plan prompt and schema in `host/worker.mjs` (search for `audience`,
  `activities`), and `docs/lesson-planning-object.md`.

## Produce

`/Users/bonorinoa/Hermes/Projects/silicus-private/plan-rubric/2026-11-14-rubric.md` (outside git; create the folder):

1. **6 to 8 criteria**, each scored 0, 1 or 2 with an **observable** anchor per score (what in the plan text shows
   it), so two judges would agree. Cover at least: each activity names who does the cognitive work; a personal attempt
   comes before any AI or external help; an AI status fits the learners' age stage; phases 1–4 all appear across the
   plan; one planned piece of evidence per session; spaced review or transfer is scheduled; multiple channels without
   "learning styles" labels (she rejects fixed styles).
2. **Judge instructions** for a blind scorer (plan text only, no model or version names).
3. **Appendix: plan schema v2 proposal**, the fields P2 would add per activity (for example phase, AI status, what
   the student does first, evidence) and per plan (spaced reviews). Fields only; no code.
4. **Credits and licence note**: ideas restated in our words, credited to Sarah Chardonnens, SYNAPSE model; no
   passage copied beyond a short phrase; the rubric stays outside the public repo until she gives written permission.

Nothing in the Silicus repository. No email. Keep it to about two pages plus the appendix.
