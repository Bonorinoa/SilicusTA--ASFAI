## In one paragraph

Silicus TA 3.0 is a course assistant in which **the AI proposes and the teacher confirms**. The code enforces
this, not just the copy. A teacher gives material and intent. A drafting model proposes objectives and a lesson
plan. The teacher accepts, renames, drops or adds each objective, confirms the plan and publishes a version. Each
checkpoint needs a recorded teacher turn in a log with a hash chain. A student helper answers only from the
published version, cites an item of it or refuses. The cite ids are checked to exist; whether the cited item
supports the answer is not checked, and the helper never sees the original material. It records nothing, and student chat is never learning evidence. ASFAI
Education is a separate protocol product. Silicus can use it and never requires it.

## Honest limits to say out loud

- **Students cannot reach it yet.** The published version stays in the teacher's browser or on the teacher's
  computer. The student space is a preview on the teacher's own device.
- **The browser and Node have different authority.** Locally, a separate process holds the record and mints the
  teacher turn. Online, that code runs in the teacher's own browser. The rules are the same code, and parity is
  tested, but the online build is a single-teacher prototype, not a multi-user service.
- **Local Node is not serverless.** Vercel functions are stateless, have time limits and use a best-effort rate
  limit per instance. The passcode is a shared secret, not a login.
- **Data.** The worker routes to zero-retention endpoints on OpenRouter unless that is turned off. Free models
  and the Anthropic wire are not zero-retention, and the app says so. Use public material only on those routes.
  YouTube works in the local app only.
- **Material storage.** "Material is never stored" is the intended invariant, not current behaviour (above).
- **Citations are not semantic checks.** Answers are package-cited, not source-grounded or validated against
  the material.
- **Live reliability.** Today the production model path fails upstream (402). Tests passing do not change that.
- **Integrity.** The log is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof. The pin on the teacher's device is the anchor.
- **Open in source:** objectives confirm automatically once the last proposal is decided (P1). A reword and back
  can seal an identical version. The 17 October evening polish and the new assessment pattern check (which also
  refuses harmless "mastery" questions) have no verification record yet.
