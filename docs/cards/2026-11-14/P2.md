## Build

1. **Schema v2**, every new field optional so a v1 plan stays valid: per activity `phase`, `supportingPhase`,
   `targetOperation`, `operationDoneBy`, `learnerFirst`, `helpAfter`, `aiStatus`, `noAiAlternative`,
   `decisionAfterHelp`, `channels`, `session`; per plan `sessions[{session, evidence:{kind, whatLearnerProduces}}]`,
   `spacedReviews[{afterDays, form, whatChanges, prompt}]`, and `aiPolicy` (one plan-level line for stages 1-2).
   Values exactly as Appendix A. No field describes a learner type.
2. **Stage is computed by the host**, never by the model (`stageForAges(ages.min)`). The normaliser **clamps** learner AI use
   the stage does not allow (stage 1: none; stage 2: teacher-led, projected, after pupils state a view; stage 3: only after a
   first try, never `required`; stage 4: any, with a record) and records each clamp in a `notes` list the teacher can see.
   A plan with no ages gets no `aiStatus` claims at all.
3. **The prompt**, in our own words: ask for the fields above; who does the thinking in each activity; a first try before any
   help; a decision after help; all four phases across the plan; one planned piece of evidence per session; at least one later
   review with a time offset and a change of context; several channels with no learning-style labels; the stage's AI rule.
   When the course has a `marbleAnchor`, include its hard prerequisites and their evidence lines as material for the opening
   activity (learners recall or bet on the prerequisite first), and ask for one spaced review that revisits the lesson in a new
   context. Untrusted-text rules stay. **Do not name SYNAPSE or Sarah Chardonnens in the prompt or the product yet** (her written
   permission is pending); a `// credit:` comment in `host/worker.mjs` naming her as the source of the design is fine.
4. Keep the single-session default (decision 2).
