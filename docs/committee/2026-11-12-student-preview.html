How seeds travel

Version 1 · published by your teacher · invented for this mock

02 · WHAT THE LESSON CONTAINS
OBJECTIVE 1Name three ways seeds travel: wind, water and animals.
OBJECTIVE 2Explain why a seed that travels away from its parent plant has a better chance to grow.
ACTIVITY 1Match a maple seed, a coconut and a burr to the way each one travels: wind, water or animals.
ACTIVITY 2Draw one seed and label how it moves.
